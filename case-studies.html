Case Study 01 · Manufacturing

Recapitalising a multi-plant industrial group

How a fragmented lender book became a unified capital platform for expansion.

Industrial · Debt & Equity

“The issue was not a lack of lenders — it was a lack of a single capital story.”

Client Situation

Five plants. Six banking relationships.

A family-owned precision manufacturer with healthy order books but a fragmented facility stack — short tenors, overlapping securities and limited headroom for a planned greenfield line.

Challenge

Maturity wall meets growth ambition

₹95 Cr of facilities were due within 14 months. Covenant tests were tightening just as management needed ₹180 Cr for capacity. Existing lenders wanted incremental security; new capital wanted a cleaner story.

Our Approach

One map. Two tracks.

We built a consolidated capital map, stress-tested cash flows under delayed offtake, and ran a dual-track process: refinance of core working capital alongside a minority equity raise earmarked for expansion.

Financial / Strategic Solution

Integrated debt-equity package

A club of relationship banks took a unified WC and term package; a growth investor subscribed preferred equity with board observer rights and a clear path to common conversion.

Outcome

₹280 Cr closed. 18-month runway secured.

Refinance completed ahead of the maturity wall. Expansion funding ring-fenced. Covenant package reset to match the new operating plan — with quarterly reporting discipline the board still uses.

Case Study 02 · Infrastructure

Project finance for a regional logistics hub

Infrastructure · Project Finance

“Limited-recourse only works when the cash waterfall is as clear as the offtake.”

Client Situation

Sponsor equity committed; debt market hesitant

A logistics platform needed limited-recourse debt for a multi-warehouse hub with long-term lease offtake from two anchor tenants — but lenders questioned construction and ramp-up risk.

Challenge

Matching structure to cash-flow timing

Standard corporate debt would over-leverage the holdco. Pure project finance required tighter offtake, cost overrun support and a credible contractor package.

Our Approach

Bankable waterfall first

We redesigned the SPV cash waterfall, introduced a contingent equity commitment and sequenced lender outreach to institutions experienced in logistics assets.

Financial / Strategic Solution

Limited-recourse facility with offtake-linked sweeps

Debt sized to contracted cash flows, with cash sweeps after DSRA funding and clear step-in rights — preserving sponsor upside on unleased capacity.

Outcome

Financial close in nine months

₹650 Cr facility executed. Construction drawdowns tracking milestones. Sponsor retained flexibility for Phase 2 without reopening core covenants.

Case Study 03 · Real Estate

Preparing a commercial portfolio for institutional capital

Real Estate · Capital Readiness

“Investors buy clarity. The assets were strong — the story was not.”

Client Situation

Strong assets. Unready reporting.

A commercial landlord with seven income-producing assets sought institutional co-investment but lacked holdco clarity, consistent valuations and debt that an investor could underwrite quickly.

Challenge

Clean-up before capital

Cross-collateralisation, related-party leases and uneven escrow practices made diligence expensive and slowed every investor conversation.

Our Approach

Institutional readiness programme

We sequenced holdco redesign, lease normalisation, debt clean-up and investor-grade reporting — treating readiness as a project with milestones, not a vague aspiration.

Financial / Strategic Solution

Portfolio refinance + governance reset

Assets moved under a clean holdco; debt refinanced against stabilised NOI; board and reporting cadence aligned with institutional expectations.

Outcome

Diligence-ready in two quarters

Sponsor entered exclusive talks with a long-term capital partner. Process cost and timeline compressed because the portfolio told one coherent story.
